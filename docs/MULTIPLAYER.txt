MALLVR - LINKED PODS (MULTIPLAYER)
==================================

In the arcades, Virtuality pods were cabled together so players could share one game.
MallVR links copies of itself the same way, in the games that linked up in the arcades:

DACTYL NIGHTMARE (4-player arena: STING, VEGA, STEALTH, BLAIZE). Pod 1 presses START,
pod 2 joins by itself. With one person per pod, the two empty seats are computer players.

DACTYL NIGHTMARE 2 links the same way as Dactyl Nightmare.

SEA DIVING links the same way too: 4 divers, two of them computer divers when two people play.

GHOST TRAIN (2 players, the red car and the blue car on one ride). It starts differently:
BOTH players press START (top button). The second press links the two pods.

BUGGY BALL (up to 4 players). Player 1 pulls the trigger on the title to start the match
for everyone; every pod picks its own car. Empty cars are driven by the computer.
(Buggy Ball is our rebuild from the game data: the original BALL.EXE is still missing.)

VIRTUALITY BOXING (2 players, head to head). The arcade's two-boxer pod ran both boxers on one
PC; MallVR does the same on pod 1's PC, and pod 2 boxes from its own headset (or window): it sends
your moves over and shows your boxer's view. Both players press START (B), then punch to pick a
boxer. Works on one PC, two PCs at home, and online.

SHOOT FOR LOOT (2 players). Each player pulls the trigger to start; you race each other
for loot, each in your own rooms (pod 1 is the master).

TAKING TURNS WITH THE COMPUTER: with fewer than 4 people, linked games alternate. The first
match is people only (head to head), the next one the computer fills the empty seats, and so
on (Dactyl Nightmare 1 and 2, Buggy Ball).

The other games were single-player in the arcades and stay that way.

How a linked Dactyl Nightmare (1 or 2) or Sea Diving game starts
   Pod 1 is the "master": its player presses START (right menu button, or 1 on the
   keyboard). Pod 2 joins by itself within a few seconds. You do not press start on
   pod 2. If pod 2 isn't ready yet, pod 1 waits up to 20 seconds for it, then plays a normal game.

PLAY ONLINE (anywhere: easiest)
   Before you start: put the Virtuality HDD dump in the games folder (Check_My_Games.bat
   says READY for Dactyl Nightmare). For the headset, turn it on and start SteamVR first.

   1. Double-click MallVR_Launcher.bat.
   2. Choose HEADSET or WINDOW, then click ONLINE.
   3. Click QUICK MATCH. It plays ANY GAME you both have (click the game name first if you
      only want one game). MallVR looks for another player who is searching too. If nobody
      is, it waits for one ("WAITING FOR ANOTHER PLAYER..."). When you're paired it counts
      down 3-2-1 and the game opens by itself. CANCEL stops looking.
   4. In the game (Dactyl Nightmare 1 or 2, Sea Diving; the others start as described at
      the top): the player whose screen says pod 1 (the host) presses START
      (headset: menu button; window: 1). The other player does NOT press start: their
      game joins by itself within a few seconds.
   5. Both see FOCUS VISETTE / WAITING FOR OPPONENT: each player pulls the trigger
      (window: Space or left click). A moment later you're both in the same arena with
      one shared scoreboard.
   6. When the game ends, close the game window: you're back on the ONLINE screen, still
      together as a group. The host presses START for another round, or clicks the game name
      first to pick another game everyone has. LEAVE (or CANCEL for the host) ends the group.

   SWITCH GAME without stopping (headset): the host holds A + B for the Button Masher Menu and
   picks SWITCH GAME, then the game. Everyone else gets GO / STAY OUT for 10 seconds (no
   answer = GO, so nobody has to let go of the controls). Every PC closes its game and opens
   the new one, linked to the same group. Everyone needs v0.11.2 or newer for this.

   Your name: you start as PLAYER482 or similar. Click the name on the ONLINE screen,
   type your own (up to 12 letters), press Enter. MallVR remembers it.

   First time only: Windows may ask whether PowerShell may use the network (that's MallVR's
   online part). Click Allow. Since v0.12.0 the games themselves (DOSBox-X) only talk to your own
   PC during online play, so they don't need to be allowed. If you said no by mistake:
   Windows Security > Firewall > Allow an app > Windows PowerShell. (LINK NETWORK, two PCs in
   one house, still needs DOSBox-X allowed on private networks.)

   Hosting and your router: nothing to set up. MallVR asks your router to open UDP port
   27200 (UPnP); if the router says no (common, and always with two routers in a row, like
   a fiber box plus your own Wi-Fi router), the two PCs "punch through" instead: each asks a
   public STUN server which address the internet sees, then both send to each other at once,
   which both routers allow. Very strict routers (some office / phone networks) can still
   block it: then let the other player host.

   MORE OPTIONS (for people who like to pick)
   Shows everyone online and every open game. HOST a game yourself, or JOIN one from the
   list; the host presses START when everyone's in. PCs in the same house find each
   other here even without internet.

   Window mode: the mouse locks into the game window to look around. TAB lets it go
   (and locks it back), ESC closes the game. More in docs\CONTROLS.txt.
   Voice chat: built in, in every linked and online game. Your mic starts ON (the headset's
   mic when Windows uses it as the recording device). Click the LEFT stick to turn it off,
   click again to turn it on (short buzz = off, long buzz = on). Window mode: M.
   Only sends while you talk. To switch it off for good: set MALLVR_VOICE=0 in Windows.

TWO PLAYERS ON ONE PC
   Double-click Link_<game>_This_PC.bat (for example Link_Dactyl_Nightmare_This_PC.bat).
   Player 1 wears the headset. Player 2 plays in a window on the monitor with the
   keyboard (J/L/I/K look, Space fire, W walk; click the window first).

TWO PCs ON THE SAME HOME NETWORK (each with MallVR, the games and a headset)
   1. On both PCs, double-click the same Link_<game>_Network.bat.
   2. It shows that PC's own address (IPv4, like 192.168.1.20). Type 1 as the pod
      number on one PC and 2 on the other, then type the OTHER PC's address.
   3. The first time, Windows asks whether DOSBox-X may use the network: allow it on
      private networks. (Uses UDP ports 27101-27104.)
   4. Start pod 2 first, then press start on pod 1.

Who is in the arena
   The arcade pods were "Duo" machines with two player places each, so a linked game
   is always 4 players. With one person per pod, the other two (VEGA and BLAIZE) are
   played by the game's own computer opponent, like in a single-player game. It's every
   player for themselves: each computer player hunts whoever is nearest, human or not.
   The red "no entry" sign is the game's own warning that you are at the edge of a
   platform and can't walk further that way.

Each pod keeps its own settings and high scores in settings\pod1, settings\pod2.

Advanced: MallVR.bat dac -Pod <1-4> [-Peers host[:port],...] [-Desktop]
Online play shows your name, the game you're in and, while you host, your internet address to other
MallVR players (that's how they connect to you). Nothing else is sent.
