MALLVR - CONTROLS
========================

The arcade pod had a headset and one hand-held joystick (the gun / hand).
Your headset is the pod headset, your RIGHT controller is the joystick.
MallVR uses SteamVR Input, so every button can be remapped in
SteamVR > Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller Bindings > MallVR.

MENU IN A GAME (the BUTTON MASHER MENU) - every controller
   PRESS AND KEEP HOLDING A + B TOGETHER (Vive wands: both menu buttons) UNTIL THE MENU APPEARS.
   A quick press does nothing: keep holding for a full second. You feel small ticks while you
   hold, then a strong buzz in both hands and the menu shows in front of you.
   RESUME = back to the game. BACK TO MENU = close the game and pick another one.
   SWITCH GAME (host of an online group) = move the whole group to another game.
   REPORT A PROBLEM = type what went wrong on the SteamVR keyboard; this game's logs go with it.
   PLAY NOTES ON / OFF = the anonymous play notes (see docs\CHANGES.txt).
   The game keeps running behind the menu, but your trigger and buttons don't reach it.
   Hold A + B again (until the buzz) to close the menu. Needs the MallVR launcher in HEADSET mode.

VALVE INDEX (tested)
   Look around ............ move your head
   Aim / punch ............ RIGHT controller
   Fire / punch ........... RIGHT trigger
   Coin + start ........... B (either hand)
   Game button ............ A (either hand) - what it does depends on the game, see GAME BUTTONS below
   Walk forward / back .... RIGHT or LEFT stick up / down
   Turn ................... RIGHT stick left / right (smooth turn, pivots on your head)
   Strafe left / right .... LEFT stick left / right
   Gun aim up / down ...... touch near the top / bottom edge of the LEFT touchpad
   Arm reach in / out ..... touch near the left / right edge of the RIGHT touchpad
   Crouch ................. hold A (Dactyl Nightmare 1 + 2)
   Voice chat ............. on by itself in linked / online games; click the LEFT stick = mic off,
                            click again = mic on (short buzz = off, long buzz = on). Window: M
   Credits ................ free play, no coins needed
   Aim and reach are saved per game, for next time (Boxing: the touchpads do nothing, so a thumb
   resting on them can't change your reach mid-fight).

HTC VIVE WANDS
   Fire ................... RIGHT trigger
   Walk / back / turn ..... RIGHT touchpad up / down / left / right (click = walk)
   Snap turn .............. grips (30 degrees)
   Gun aim / arm reach .... LEFT touchpad up-down / left-right
   Coin + start ........... RIGHT menu button
   Game button ............ LEFT menu button (crouch in Dactyl, turbo in Buggy Ball, Boxing special)

GAME BUTTONS - the extra controls each game had on the pod
   The arcade joystick had a trigger plus a top/thumb button; some games used them together.
   Dactyl Nightmare 1 + 2 . hold A to crouch (window: hold C)
   Buggy Ball ............. A = turbo (the right grip still works too). Window: Space or right mouse
   Virtuality Boxing ...... SPECIAL MOVE: when your special bar is full and flashing, get close and
                            press A, or pull either trigger (window: Space). Each boxer has his own
                            special. Punches don't need any button.
   Total Recoil ........... left trigger flips free / assisted aim; A, B or stick down = menu next

TWO-PLAYER BOXING (Link_Virtuality_Boxing_This_PC.bat / _Network.bat, or LINKED in the launcher)
   Head to head like the arcade's two-seat pod: pod 1's PC runs both boxers, pod 2 boxes from its own
   headset (or window). Both press start (B), then punch to pick your boxer.

META QUEST / OCULUS TOUCH (through SteamVR: Quest Link, Air Link or Virtual Desktop)
   Fire / punch ........... RIGHT trigger
   Coin + start ........... B
   Game button ............ A or X (crouch in Dactyl, turbo in Buggy Ball, Boxing special)
   Walk / turn / strafe ... the sticks, same as the Index (above)
   Mode ................... LEFT trigger (Total Recoil aim mode; Pac-Man: walk back)
   Voice chat ............. click the LEFT stick = mic off / on
   ADJUST (gun aim, reach). HOLD Y: while you hold it you stand still and the sticks adjust instead:
      LEFT stick up / down .... gun aim up / down (2 degrees per push)
      RIGHT stick left / right  arm reach in / out (Total Recoil: moves the gun)
      Push, let the stick come back to the middle, push again for the next step. Saved per game.
   If an older MallVR binding was saved for your Quest, pick "MallVR - Meta Quest / Oculus Touch"
   in SteamVR > Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller Bindings > MallVR.

KEYBOARD ADJUSTERS IN VR (any headset: click the MallVR game window first, so it has the keyboard)
   Up / Down arrow ........ gun aim up / down
   Left / Right arrow ..... arm reach in / out
   VIRTUALITY BOXING (the controllers can't change these, so a thumb can't bump them mid-fight):
      Right arrow ......... punches land with a SHORTER jab (try this first if your hits don't count)
      Left arrow .......... a LONGER jab is needed
      Up / Down arrow ..... right arm higher / lower
      [ and ] ............. gloves closer together / wider apart
   Every press gives a short buzz in both hands and is saved for next time.

PAC-MAN VR
   Walk forward ........... RIGHT trigger (or a stick up, as before)
   Walk back .............. LEFT trigger (or a stick down)

Other controllers: SteamVR picks the closest layout; remap there if needed.

NO HEADSET / WINDOW MODE (Play_<Game>_NoVR.bat, or WINDOW in MallVR_Launcher.bat)
   Every game also runs in a normal window with keyboard and mouse.

   THE MOUSE (read this first)
   - Click inside the game window once. From then on the mouse turns your view and the
     pointer is held in the window (that's normal: it's how you look around).
   - TAB (or a middle click) = let go of the mouse, so you can use other windows.
     Press TAB again (or middle click) to lock it back in for looking around.
   - Hold ALT = the mouse is free only while you hold it.
   - To switch to another program: press TAB first, then click the other window
     (or use Alt+Tab). Click back into the game window to play again.
   - ESC CLOSES THE GAME straight away. Don't press it to "get the mouse back": use TAB.
   - The game only listens while its window is the active one. Two windows on one PC
     (linked play): click the window you want to control.

   KEYS
   Coin ................... 5
   Start .................. 1
   Walk forward ........... W (or hold the right mouse button)
   Step back / strafe ..... S / A and D
   Turn / look ............ move the mouse (or J / L left-right, I / K up-down)
   Aim the gun / punch .... arrow keys (R = gun back to centre)
   Fire ................... left mouse button or Space
   Crouch ................. hold C (Dactyl Nightmare 1 + 2)
   Quit ................... Esc, or close the window
   Ghost Train ............ W moves the car (the pod's top button)
   Missile Command ........ A / D move the gun left and right along its track
   Virtuality Boxing ...... punch with EITHER controller: the hand you push out toward
                            the opponent throws that glove (guard = hands up in front, nothing
                            extends). Face the opponent. You move by LEANING, like the arcade pod:
                            stand where you want to box, then press coin / start (that spot becomes
                            the centre). Lean forward / back / to the sides to step, or hold a stick
                            to lean. Play standing.
                            WINDOW MODE: left mouse button = left punch, right mouse button = right
                            punch, Space = special move, W / S / A / D = lean.
